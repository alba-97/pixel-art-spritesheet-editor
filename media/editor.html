Tool

Shift: draw a straight line.

Shift: snap to horizontal, vertical or 45°. Alt: draw from the center.

Shift: square/circle. Alt: draw from the center.

Drag to select. Shift: square. Alt: from the center. Drag inside the selection to move it. Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V / Del, arrows to nudge.

Drag to select an ellipse. Shift: circle. Alt: from the center.

Drag the selection (or the whole image) as floating content. Enter applies it, Esc cancels.

Drag to pan the canvas. You can also hold Space and drag, or use the middle mouse button.

Click to pick the primary color. Right-click for the secondary.

Click to select the contiguous area of the exact same color. Drawing tools only affect pixels inside the selection.
